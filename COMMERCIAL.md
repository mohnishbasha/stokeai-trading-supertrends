## What counts as noncommercial

Noncommercial purposes include:

- Personal use, including running your own instance for your own analysis.
- Personal study, private research, and hobby projects.
- Academic and scientific research.
- Use by charitable organizations, educational institutions, public research
  organizations, public safety or health organizations, environmental
  protection organizations, and government institutions, regardless of the
  source of funding.

## Examples that need a commercial license

- Selling the software, or selling access to it.
- Offering it, or its output, as a paid or hosted service.
- Using it inside a for-profit business, including for the business's own
  trading, research, or client work.
- Bundling it into a commercial product or service.
- Publishing or distributing its picks to others in exchange for payment,
  advertising revenue, or other commercial benefit.

If you are unsure which side your use falls on, ask before you start.

## How to ask

Email legal@serverlessvc.com. Please include:

- Your name, your organization, and its legal entity type.
- What you want to do with the software.
- Who would use it and roughly how many users.
- Whether you would modify it or redistribute it.
- The timeline you have in mind.
