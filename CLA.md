## How to sign

Sign by commenting on Your pull request with exactly this sentence:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

A CLA bot (the contributor-assistant GitHub action) records the signature in
`signatures/cla.json` on the `cla-signatures` branch. You sign once, and the
signature covers Your later Contributions to the Project. Pull requests from
contributors who have not signed are blocked until they do.
