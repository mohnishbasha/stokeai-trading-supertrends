# Disclaimer

> Super Trends output is general market analysis generated by an algorithm from technical and fundamental data. It is not investment advice, not a recommendation tailored to you, and not an offer to buy or sell any security. The algorithm can be and often is wrong. Past and backtested performance does not predict future results. You are responsible for your own decisions.

## What this project is

- It is software for running your own analysis. It is not a service.
- It provides no signals service. The project does not publish picks.
- Each user needs their own market data license. The project distributes no
  market data, and a vendor's terms for display and redistribution depend on
  the plan you hold.
- It is not a broker. It has no brokerage integration, holds no brokerage
  credentials, and places no orders.
- It does not ask for or store your holdings, account size, risk tolerance, or
  goals.

Before giving anyone else access to a running instance, sharing its picks
publicly, or charging for them, talk to a securities lawyer. Publishing buy and
sell signals to others can bring investment adviser rules into play.
